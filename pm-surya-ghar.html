Simple 4-Step Process

How Step Solar handles your Direct Bank Transfer (DBT)

You don't need to visit government or DISCOM offices. Our liaison and technical engineers manage the paperwork end-to-end.

01

Portal Registration

We register your electricity connection on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal and verify your bank account details for DBT.

02

Technical Feasibility

We perform the physical site survey, calculate shadow-free space, and obtain technical approval from your local DISCOM.

03

Installation & Net Meter

Our certified technicians install ALMM Tier-1 panels and coordinate DISCOM inspection for bi-directional net meter installation.

04

Direct Bank Transfer

Discom commissions the system. Both Central DBT and State subsidies (up to ₹1,08,000) are credited straight to your bank account!
