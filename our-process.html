End-To-End Transparency

Seven Steps, One Dedicated Team Member at Each.

Every solar installation moves through defined, transparent stages handled by specialized professionals. From initial electricity bill assessment to final net-meter commissioning and DBT subsidy deposit, we keep you informed at every milestone.

01
Sales Consultation Team

Enquiry & Requirement Call

You share your electricity bill and roof/land details via website, phone or WhatsApp. Our sales engineer analyzes your historical consumption and calculates recommended capacity.

  • Review average monthly electricity consumption
  • Explain Central & State subsidy entitlements
  • Provide instant estimated cost and savings overview
02
Site Survey Engineering Team

Free Physical Site Survey

Our solar surveyor visits your property to conduct a detailed physical inspection without any cost or obligation.

  • Accurate 3D roof shadow analysis and orientation measurement
  • Check structural load-bearing capacity of roof / terrace
  • Inspect existing electrical wiring, distribution board & earthing
03
Design & Engineering Team

Custom 3D Design & Itemised Quotation

We generate a precise technical CAD layout showing exact panel placements and send you an itemised quotation in PDF format.

  • Itemised breakdown: panels, inverter, GI structures, wiring, safety hardware
  • Expected monthly & annual generation modeling
  • Complete transparent price with zero hidden clauses
04
Accounts & Liaison Team

Booking & PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Filing

Upon quotation approval, our liaison specialist registers your consumer account on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal and initiates DISCOM technical feasibility approvals.

  • National Portal registration & bank account linkage
  • Submit DISCOM net-metering application
  • Lock in central & state subsidy reservation
05
Certified Installation Technicians

Material Dispatch & Mechanical Installation

Factory-sealed Tier-1 solar modules, smart inverters, and heavy hot-dip galvanized mounting structures are dispatched to your site and installed by trained technicians.

  • Secure structure anchoring engineered for 150 km/h wind loads
  • Precision DC cabling with UV-resistant conduits and MC4 connectors
  • Chemical earthing pits and lightning arrester installation
06
Technical & Grid Engineers

Testing & DISCOM Net-Metering Commissioning

Our electrical engineers test the complete circuit end-to-end and coordinate with DISCOM officials for bidirectional net-meter testing, seal verification, and final grid synchronization.

  • Comprehensive insulation, polarity, and anti-islanding safety tests
  • Installation and testing of bi-directional net meter
  • Plant switch-on and initial power generation verification
07
Accounts & Customer Success Team

Handover, Warranty & DBT Subsidy Credit

We hand over your project completion certificate, 25-year panel warranty cards, inverter login credentials, and finalize DBT subsidy release directly into your bank account.

  • Mobile app setup for live daily generation tracking
  • Final invoice with GST documentation for tax / accounts
  • Subsidies (up to ₹1,08,000) credited via Direct Bank Transfer

What documents do you need to get started?

We make it effortless. You only need to share photos or copies of the following 4 documents:

📄

Latest Electricity Bill

🪪

Aadhaar Card

For KYC verification on the PM Surya Ghar National Portal.

🏦

Bank Passbook / Cheque

For direct deposit of Central and State DBT subsidies into your account.

🏠

A quick photo of your open roof to help us evaluate shadow-free space.
