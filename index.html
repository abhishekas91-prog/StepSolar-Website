FAQ

Solar panel installation questions, answered.

What is the price of a rooftop solar system?

A rooftop solar system typically costs about ₹55,000 per kW installed. A 3 kW system therefore works out to roughly ₹1.65 lakh before subsidy. Your exact quote is confirmed after a free site survey, and we itemise every cost — panels, inverter, structure, wiring and labour — with no hidden charges.

How much can I save on my electricity bill with solar panels?

Most Step Solar customers reduce their monthly electricity bill by up to 90%. With net metering, surplus energy your rooftop generates is exported back to the grid and adjusted against the power you draw at night.

How long does a solar system take to pay for itself?

Typical payback is 4–5 years for most homes, while solar panels carry a 25-year performance warranty — so you keep saving for two decades after the system has paid for itself.

Do I have to handle the subsidy and net-metering paperwork myself?

No. Step Solar manages your PM Surya Ghar subsidy and discom net-metering paperwork for you, so you don't have to run between government offices.

Which areas does Step Solar serve?

We install and service rooftop solar across Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, and are based in Bhadaura, Ghazipur (UP).

Is a site visit required before I get a quotation?

Yes — a free, no-obligation site survey is the first step. Our surveyor checks your roof or land space, shadow-free area, structure strength and electrical connection, and only then we size your system and share an itemised quotation.
