Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about costs, subsidies, net metering and installation.

What is the price of a rooftop solar system?

A rooftop solar system typically costs about ₹55,000 per kW installed. A 3 kW system therefore works out to roughly ₹1.65 lakh before subsidy. With PM Surya Ghar Central DBT (₹78,000) and State Top-Up (₹30,000), your total subsidy can reach ₹1,08,000, bringing your net investment down to ~₹57,000.

How much can I save on my electricity bill with solar panels?

Most Step Solar customers reduce their monthly electricity bill by up to 90%. With net metering, surplus energy your rooftop generates during sunny hours is exported back to the grid and adjusted against the power you draw at night.

How long does a solar system take to pay for itself?

Typical payback is 2–3 years with full central and state subsidies, while solar panels carry a 25-year performance warranty — so you enjoy free, clean electricity for over two decades after the system has fully paid for itself.

Do I have to handle the subsidy and net-metering paperwork myself?

No. Step Solar manages your complete PM Surya Ghar registration, technical feasibility approvals, and DISCOM bi-directional net-metering paperwork for you. The subsidy is deposited directly into your bank account via DBT.

Which areas does Step Solar serve?

We install and service rooftop solar and solar water pumps across Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, with our registered headquarters based in Satramganj Bazar, Bhadaura, Ghazipur (UP).

Is a site visit required before I get a quotation?

Yes — a free, no-obligation site survey is the first step. Our surveyor inspects your roof space, shadow-free orientation, structure strength, and existing electrical connection, and only then do we issue an itemised quotation.
